# Worldkiln Founding Developer Program

The Worldkiln Founding Developer Program recognizes developers who help shape Worldkiln during its early development.

Founding Developers are people who meaningfully use the engine, build projects, test releases, report problems, provide feedback, or otherwise contribute to improving Worldkiln before version 1.0.

## Founding Developer Status

Founding Developer status is intended to be permanent.

When Worldkiln eventually moves beyond its founding era, the developers who earned that status remain part of its history.

## Participation

The program is based on meaningful involvement rather than registration alone.

Participation may include:

- Building projects with Worldkiln.
- Testing public or development releases.
- Reporting bugs and regressions.
- Providing product or workflow feedback.
- Helping improve documentation or usability.
- Contributing useful examples, ideas, or testing.

There is no requirement that every Founding Developer contribute in the same way.

## Benefits

Founding Developers may receive special recognition, access, opportunities, previews, community roles, or other benefits associated with Worldkiln.

The exact benefits may evolve as Worldkiln grows.

Founding Developer status does not guarantee any specific product, service, payment, discount, access level, or commercial benefit unless separately announced.

## Founding Era

The Founding Developer Program is tied to Worldkiln's pre-1.0 development period.

The founding era will eventually close.

Developers who earned Founding Developer status before that point will retain it.
